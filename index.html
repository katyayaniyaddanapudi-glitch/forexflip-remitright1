Frequently asked questions

What is a currency converter?

A currency converter calculates the value of one currency in another using an exchange rate.

Are the exchange rates live?

When ForexFlip runs on a normal website or local server, rates come from the open.er-api.com service and are cached for 1 hour. If that service is unavailable, the app falls back to built-in sample rates. The line under the converter always shows which source is in use.

What is the mid-market rate, and why is it not what I receive?

The mid-market rate is the midpoint between the buy and sell price of a currency. Transfer providers add a spread to that rate and often charge a fee on top, so the recipient gets less. ForexFlip shows that gap for each provider.

How is the recipient amount calculated?

Recipient amount = (amount you send − provider fee) × mid-market rate × (1 − spread). In “They receive” mode, the app works backwards to show how much you need to send.

Are the provider fees and spreads real quotes?

No. They are illustrative figures for comparison. Always check the provider’s own quote before you send money.

How do rate alerts work?

Click the bell, enter a target rate and choose whether you want the rate to rise above it or fall below it. You get an on-screen notice, and a browser notification if you allow it, when the rate reaches your target.

Is ForexFlip free?

Yes. It is free to use and needs no sign-up.
